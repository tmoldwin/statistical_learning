## Session — demo vocab switch to eight-word -ate/-at

### Questions
- Can a small demo show clear multi-prefix DFA clustering while keeping DFA ≠ position-from-beginning?
- Promote that vocab as the paper/slides narrative demo?

### Experiments / controls
Tried then rejected:
- `five_word_ake_demo` (*bake…sake*): perfect 5×5 DFA blocks but DFA ≡ position.
- `five_word_ate_chain_demo` (*ate, late, plate, slate, gate*): DFA ≠ pos-begin, but single suffix family; pos-end ≈ DFA.

Promoted:
- **`eight_word_ate_at_demo_ns`**: *ate, late, plate, slate, gate, cat, hat, mat*
  - H=50, seeds 1,2,3,5,7,8; 30k chars; early-stop ≤3% word error
  - ~27 condensed prefixes; minimized DFA = 8 states

### Results
Seed-mean feature separation (6 seeds):
- DFA η² ≈ **0.88**, within-corr ≈ 0.87, silhouette ≈ 0.59
- Position-from-beginning η² ≈ **0.38** (distinct from DFA)
- Word η² ≈ **0.06**

Correlation / PCA: multi-prefix DFA blocks (e.g. `a,ga,la,pla,sla` and `at,gat,lat,plat,slat`) plus separate -at family mid-states (`ca,ha,ma`); accept states from both families merge.

### Changes shipped
- Removed Minimal-DFA diagram from PCA/separation composite (`plot_dfa_pca_and_separation_combined`).
- Paper collect `DEMO` → `eight_word_ate_at_demo_ns`; draft + slides rebuilt.
- Word-decoding plots registered under `plots/decoding/`.
